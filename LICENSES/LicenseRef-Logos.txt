Logos and wordmarks

The image files under docs/assets/ and the TEI Consortium mark shipped as
src/opm/resources/document/tei-logo.svg (source:
https://tei-c.org/about/logos/TEI_Logo.svg) are logos and wordmarks: those of
e-editiones and Open Processing Model, the TEI Consortium, and those of the
institutions that support the project's development — among them the
Schweizerische Akademie der Geistes- und Sozialwissenschaften, the
Universität Basel, the Universität Zürich, the Zentralbibliothek Zürich and the
Ernst Göhner Stiftung.
