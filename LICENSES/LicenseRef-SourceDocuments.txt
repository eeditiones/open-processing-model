Source documents

The files under examples/*/data/ are scholarly source texts — journal articles,
correspondence, dramatic works and their editorial apparatus — used to demonstrate what
Open Processing Model does. They are not part of the software, and e-editiones does not
claim copyright in them.

Each carries its own provenance and rights, stated in the README of the example project
it belongs to. The CC0 dedication that covers the surrounding scaffolding (section 4 of
LICENSE-EXCEPTIONS.md) does not extend to them, and neither does the AGPL that covers opm
itself.

If you reuse one of these documents outside this repository, check the example's README
for its source and terms first.
